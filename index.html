02 / Our services

Care for what matters.

Clear explanations for common treatments, so you know what a visit may involve and what questions to bring.

01 / Restore and preserveRoot canal

Root canal treatment

Treatment for an inflamed or infected tooth nerve, with the aim of easing discomfort and preserving the natural tooth when possible.

View treatment costs ↗
02 / Align and guideOrthodontics

Braces and alignment

Orthodontic options can help guide tooth position and bite over time. A consultation helps determine which approach suits you.

View treatment costs ↗
03 / Remove with careOral surgery

Tooth extraction

When a tooth cannot be restored, removal may be recommended. Your dentist will explain the options and aftercare before treatment.

View treatment costs ↗
04 / Protect and restoreRestorative care

Crowns and bridges

Custom restorations can help protect a weakened tooth or replace a missing one. The right design depends on an examination.

View treatment costs ↗
05 / Clean and maintainPreventive care

Cleaning and polishing

A professional cleaning removes built-up deposits and helps support healthy gums. Your dentist can suggest a suitable review schedule.

View treatment costs ↗
06 / Replace a missing toothImplant care

Dental implants

An implant can support a replacement tooth. Suitability, timing and maintenance are discussed after a full clinical assessment.

View treatment costs ↗
07 / Brighten your smileCosmetic care

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening options can brighten natural teeth. A dental check first helps confirm whether whitening is appropriate for you.

View treatment costs ↗
08 / Care from the startChildren’s dentistry

Children’s dental care

Gentle check-ups help children get comfortable with dental visits and build healthy brushing habits as their teeth grow.
